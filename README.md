**Vendor Performance Analysis Dashboard**

1.**Project Headline**

**Vendor Performance Analysis Dashboard | Power BI | Procurement & Supply Chain Analytics**

---

2. **Project Overview**

The **Vendor Performance Analysis Dashboard** is an interactive Business Intelligence solution developed in **Power BI** to evaluate supplier performance using key procurement metrics such as purchase value, delivery performance, product quality, lead time, vendor reliability, payment status, and procurement costs. The dashboard transforms procurement data into actionable insights, enabling organizations to optimize vendor selection, reduce procurement risks, improve supply chain efficiency, and strengthen supplier relationships.

---

3.**Project Purpose**

The primary objective of this dashboard is to provide procurement managers and business stakeholders with a centralized platform to monitor vendor performance, identify top-performing suppliers, reduce procurement costs, improve delivery efficiency, and make strategic sourcing decisions based on data.

---

4. **Business Problem**

Organizations often work with multiple vendors, making it challenging to:

* Identify the most reliable suppliers.
* Monitor vendor delivery performance.
* Compare procurement costs across vendors.
* Track purchase orders and payment status.
* Evaluate supplier quality and defect rates.
* Detect delayed deliveries and procurement bottlenecks.
* Improve supplier relationships and contract negotiations.

This dashboard addresses these challenges by consolidating procurement data into a single interactive reporting solution.

---

5.**Project Objectives**

* Evaluate vendor performance using key procurement KPIs.
* Monitor purchase orders and procurement spending.
* Compare vendors based on cost, quality, and delivery.
* Analyze supplier lead times and on-time delivery rates.
* Identify high-performing and underperforming vendors.
* Improve procurement efficiency through interactive reporting.
* Support data-driven supplier selection and contract management.
